# Methodology

## Where the numbers come from

Every figure on this site comes from **a sworn affidavit** that each candidate
filed with the Election Commission of India (ECI) before the 2024 Andhra Pradesh
assembly election.

Candidates are legally required to disclose their education, criminal cases,
assets, and liabilities. These affidavits are public record.

We pull the data from two places:

1. **[MyNeta](https://www.myneta.info/AndhraPradesh2024/)** — a project by the
   Association for Democratic Reforms (ADR) that digitises and publishes every
   candidate's affidavit in a searchable format.
2. **ECI affidavits** — the original filings from the Election Commission of
   India.

Where ADR's published data and the ECI's original affidavit disagree, **the ECI
version is authoritative**. ADR itself says so on their site.

---

## What "declared criminal cases" means

When we say an MLA has **declared criminal cases**, we mean exactly this:

> The candidate disclosed pending criminal cases against them in their
> sworn affidavit to the Election Commission.

That is a fact about what they declared. It is not a judgment about guilt.

**Pending cases are allegations, not convictions.** Many cases in Indian politics
are politically motivated, filed across party lines, and remain pending for
years. A pending case does not mean someone did something wrong — it means a case
exists in court.

We show three separate numbers for every representative:

- **Total declared criminal cases** — all pending cases the candidate listed.
- **Serious declared criminal cases** — cases where the maximum punishment is
  5 years or more in prison. This is a standard category defined by ADR, not our
  invention.
- **Convictions** — cases where a court has found the person guilty. This is
  always shown separately and is never mixed into the total count.

We describe only what was declared, in the candidate's own affidavit language —
never a label for the person, and never a rate or aggregate summary of it.

---

## What "declared assets" means

Asset figures — movable, immovable, total, and liabilities — are **exactly as
the candidate wrote them** in their affidavit. We do not estimate, adjust for
inflation, or editorialize.

Amounts are in Indian Rupees (₹). We display them in crores (₹1 crore =
₹10,000,000) or lakhs (₹1 lakh = ₹100,000) for readability.

---

## Photos

We only use photos that are **freely licensed** — from Wikimedia Commons,
Wikipedia, or official sources that permit reuse. Each photo includes a credit
and license tag.

If no freely licensed photo exists for a representative, we show their initials
instead. We do not use news-agency photos, social media images, or any image
without clear licensing.

---

## How we handle corrections

We're human. If something is wrong, we want to know.

Every representative's profile page has a **Report a correction** link. It goes
to [corrections@ssupwithandhra.com](mailto:corrections@ssupwithandhra.com).

When you report a correction:

1. Tell us which constituency and what looks wrong.
2. If you can, point us to the ECI affidavit or official source that shows the
   correct figure.
3. We'll verify against the original affidavit and update the record.

Every change we make is tracked in our public
[git history](https://github.com/bhargavbvs/supwithstates) — so you can see
exactly what changed, when, and why.
