# About

**ssup with Telangana** is a plain lookup: find your assembly constituency, see
who represents it, and read what they declared about themselves on oath.

Everything here is self-declared. Candidates file a sworn affidavit with the
Election Commission of India before an election, listing their education, their
assets and liabilities, and any criminal cases pending against them. Those
filings are public. This site puts them where a voter can actually find them —
by constituency, on a map, in about two taps.

## What it covers

- **119 assembly constituencies**, each with the member elected in December 2023.
- **17 Lok Sabha seats**, each with the member elected in May 2024, and the
  assembly segments inside them.
- The same fields for both: declared cases, assets, education, age, profession.

## What it is not

It is not a verdict. A pending case is an allegation; a declared asset is a
declaration. This site reports what was filed, with a link to the filing, and
leaves the conclusion to you.

It is also not complete. Where a figure is not published, the page says so
instead of guessing — see the [methodology](#/telangana/methodology) for exactly
what is missing and why.

## Corrections

Something wrong? Every profile has a correction link, and every change is in the
public [git history](https://github.com/bhargavbvs/supwithstates).
