# About

## What is this?

A free, non-commercial site that shows who represents your assembly constituency
in {{state}} — and what they declared about themselves before they were elected.

Every member filed a sworn affidavit with the Election Commission before the
{{year}} election, covering their education, declared criminal cases, assets and
liabilities. It is public record. We made it easier to find and read.

{{state}} has {{assembly_size}} assembly seats, and {{profiled}} of them are on
this site.

## Why does this exist?

Because reading your own representative's public record should not require
digging through government PDFs. The information is already out there, scattered
across ECI filings and ADR reports. This puts it in one place, on a map, in
plain language, with every number linked back to its source.

## Who runs this?

An independent, unfunded, non-commercial project run by a solo volunteer. **The
maintainer is not affiliated with, employed by, or funded by any political
party, candidate, government body, media house or NGO.** It carries no
advertising and does not endorse, oppose, rank or score any representative or
party.

## What this site is not

- **Not a news site.** No opinions, editorials or breaking news.
- **Not a rating system.** Nobody is scored or ranked. Sorting a list by what a
  member declared is a claim about the affidavit, never about the person.
- **Not partisan.** Every member is shown the same way, from the same sources.

## Found an error?

Email [{{corrections}}](mailto:{{corrections}}). We take corrections seriously:
if something is wrong we fix it, and the change shows up in the public commit
history.
